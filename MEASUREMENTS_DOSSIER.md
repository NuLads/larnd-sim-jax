# Measurement dossier — LArTPC differentiable calibration

Raw measurements only. No interpretation, no diagnosis, no conclusions. All numbers are from
completed fits or direct numerical evaluation.

## 1. The system

A JAX differentiable simulation of a liquid-argon TPC. Charge deposits along particle tracks drift
to a pixel plane, inducing currents that are digitized by a self-triggering front-end (threshold
5000 e-, reset noise sigma 900 e-, uncorrelated noise 500 e-).

**Data**: through-going cosmic muons, ~4000 track segments per fit batch, 100 batches per epoch.
Targets are generated by the stochastic simulation (with electronics noise throws) at known
parameter values; the fit uses a probabilistic forward model that marginalizes the noise
analytically. Fits use Adam on a marked Poisson point-process negative log-likelihood.

**Quantities fitted (three groups)**:
- 5 global calibration parameters: `Ab` (recombination), `eField`, `lifetime` (electron lifetime,
  charge attenuates as exp(-t_drift/lifetime)), `tran_diff`, `long_diff` (diffusion constants).
  Optimized in a bounded sigmoid parametrization; ranges e.g. lifetime [400, 6000] us,
  Ab [0.75, 0.9].
- ~4000 per-segment `dEdx` nuisance parameters (one per track segment), with a student-t prior,
  a soft barrier, and a mean constraint (weight 1e5).
- Per-track geometry: each track's shape is fitted as transverse sine-mode displacements
  ("spline basis", K~3-12 modes per track, endpoints anchored) applied to a chain of nodes every
  2 cm. Segment length dx is held fixed.

**Track geometry statistics** (measured from the sample): per-track span along the drift
coordinate: median 12.5 cm, p90 50 cm, max 61 cm. Whole-sample drift-coordinate range: 61 cm.
Drift velocity 0.16 cm/us; with lifetime 2400 us the attenuation length is ~385 cm. "400 cm" in
run labels refers to the maximum batch arc length, not drift span.

## 4. Trajectory measurements (S4)

- Variance-of-increment Var(x[t+tau] - x[t])/tau for the lifetime trajectory rises with lag,
  peaks around tau ~ 20-50 iterations, then falls by a factor 3-4 by tau = 1000.
- Per-step lifetime gradient: no persistent sign bias in any time window (mean/std 0.02-0.3,
  ~50% negative fraction), in both S4 and S2.
- Late-training parameter step sizes in one 8000-iteration run: RMS ~31 over iterations
  7200-8000 versus RMS ~14 over iterations 0-800.
- FFT of detrended lifetime trajectories shows peaks at periods ~100-250 iterations. Averaging
  synchronously with the 100-batch epoch cycle shows a fixed batch-position effect accounts for
  0.6%-7% of within-epoch variance.
- Autocorrelation of the lifetime gradient has a local peak at lag = 100 (= number of batches),
  value 0.25-0.55 depending on run.
- Step-to-step increment correlations (late phase, S4): Ab-lifetime +0.81 to +0.94;
  Ab-tran_diff -0.46 to -0.70; tran_diff-long_diff +0.44 to +0.55; eField uncorrelated with all
  (|r| < 0.10).
- Final-error correlation across the 6 seeds: lifetime vs long_diff r = 0.80.
- Per-segment dEdx mean absolute error during S4 with prior weight 0.5: grows monotonically
  0.035 -> 0.137 MeV/cm over 5000 iterations. With prior weight 5: stays flat at ~0.04, while
  lifetime error still reaches +90% in one seed.
- Geometry residual during S4: 786 um at iteration 0-500, 549 um at ~2500, 528 um at 5000
  (baseline config).
- Correlation of lifetime with number of active pixels, matched hits, or unmatched-hit penalty:
  |r| < 0.15 in all runs checked.

## 5. Learning-rate schedules (as actually implemented)

- Calibration parameters: `warmup_exponential_decay_schedule`, peak 1e-1, warmup 500 iterations,
  decay_rate 0.999, `transition_steps` = number of batches (100), `staircase=True`. Evaluated:
  iteration 500 -> 100% of peak; 2000 -> 98.5%; 5000 -> 95.6%; 8000 -> 92.8%; 10000 -> 90.9%.
- Geometry (chain) parameters: multiplicative factor `chain_decay_rate ** iteration` applied per
  iteration. With 0.999: 0.082 at iteration 2500, 0.0067 at 5000, 0.00033 at 8000. With 0.9997:
  0.47 at 2500, 0.22 at 5000, 0.091 at 8000.
- Per-segment dEdx: fixed learning rate 1e-2, no decay.

## 7. Second-order optimizer measurements

- Full-batch damped Newton on the 5 calibration parameters, started from nominal values: the
  accumulated loss decreases monotonically (294825 -> 257434 over 40 steps) while the lifetime
  error grows from +7% to +1003% and long_diff reaches -94%. Parameters reach their range bounds.
- Reference likelihood values on the same data: at nominal start 299826; at true parameters 76627;
  at the Adam 5000-iteration solution 77570.
- The same Newton method warm-started from the Adam solution at iteration 1000 converges in 2-3
  steps and stops itself, reaching: Ab -0.09%, eField -0.00%, lifetime -1.03%, tran_diff +0.55%,
  long_diff -12.24% (Adam at iteration 5000 on the same data: Ab -0.0%, eField -0.1%,
  lifetime -0.6%, tran_diff -1.2%, long_diff -19.2%).
- A Gauss-Newton/Fisher approximation of the curvature differs from the exact Hessian by
  relative Frobenius norm 1.75-4.27 evaluated at truth, and the discrepancy does not shrink when
  the number of batches is increased from 1 to 7 (the Ab diagonal ratio grows 1.74 -> 4.88).
- Second-order geometry fitting (matrix-free Gauss-Newton with conjugate gradients): 19 iterations
  completed in 3.3 hours versus ~2500 iterations for first-order Adam in the same time.
  An earlier noiseless position-only comparison gave 107 um (Gauss-Newton) vs 51 um (Adam).

## 8. Other measurements

- Position-only fits (calibration and dEdx fixed) reach a per-track residual of 51 um at 50 cm
  track length, 148 um at 100 cm; binned by individual track length the median residual is
  39 um at 15 cm, 68 um at 50 cm, 109 um at 70 cm, 206 um at 95 cm.
- The same spline basis can represent the true track shape to 26 um (least-squares fit to truth).
  The purely statistical component of the position residual is ~9 um.
- Cost: a 5000-iteration S4 fit at 400 cm takes ~6.7 hours on an A100. A 5000-iteration S2 fit
  takes ~3 hours. Jitting the loss+gradient gives a 2.6x per-iteration speedup after a ~10 minute
  compile.
- Under an earlier configuration in which the target contained no electronics noise while the
  fit model still marginalized over noise, S2 long_diff was -19.2%; with the noise made
  consistent the same fit gives +12.6%.
- Halving the discrimination threshold (5000 -> 2500 e-) in S2: lifetime +29.4% (seed0),
  +1.1% (seed1), versus -0.3% and +3.3% at the nominal threshold.
